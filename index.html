About this data

This is a convenience sample (friends, family and schools reached online), not a random sample of Lesotho, so the percentages describe these respondents only. Self-reported traits can be misjudged, and small groups give noisy percentages. Many of these traits are often taught as simple dominant/recessive traits, but their genetics is more complicated, so this tool shows frequencies, not inheritance. Emails and timestamps were removed before publishing. The age question was added after the first responses came in, and some respondents’ sex was not recoverable from the export, which is why those rows appear only under “All”.
